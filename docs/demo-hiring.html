A hiring platform, configured and classified

One real run against a live service: a domain created from a one-line brief, an applicant tracking system's tools registered over MCP, 18 intents with 152 examples indexed, then a conversation classified against them. Every number here came out of the run.

1

The domain

An administrator supplies a name and a sentence. The role and vocabulary the extractor works from are drafted from those and saved.

hiring

an applicant tracking system: open requisitions, search and shortlist candidates, schedule interviews, collect scorecards, make offers and report on the pipeline

System instructions, drafted

You are acting as an HR and people-operations assistant. This is the hiring domain, which covers an applicant tracking system: open requisitions, search and shortlist candidates, schedule interviews, collect scorecards, make offers and report on the pipeline. Interpret every request in that role and use only the terminology the request itself contains. Never infer a value that is not stated; when an entity is not mentioned, leave it out rather than guessing.

User instructions, drafted

Users are HR staff and employees asking about themselves or colleagues, working with an applicant tracking system: open requisitions, search and shortlist candidates, schedule interviews, collect scorecards, make offers and report on the pipeline. They phrase requests informally, name things by their common business terms, and often refer back to items from earlier in the conversation.

Both are ordinary configuration: editable on the domain page, and layered onto every extraction prompt in this domain.
